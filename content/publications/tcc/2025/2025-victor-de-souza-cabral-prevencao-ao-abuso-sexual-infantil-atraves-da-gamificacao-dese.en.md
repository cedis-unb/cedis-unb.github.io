## Abstract

Proposes gamification as a motivational strategy to bring children the content of the two main booklets of the Eu Me Protejo project on preventing child sexual abuse. The result is a gamified mobile application for children aged 4 to 10, used with the mediation of parents, guardians or educators. The methodology combined action research with specialists, requirements definition and development using gamification practices and plain language, aligned with the project's materials.
