## Abstract

Develops an offline gamified mobile application based on the good-practices manual of the Pró-vida e Eu Me Protejo project, a partnership between the Pró-Vida institution and the Eu Me Protejo project. The app brings childhood-violence prevention content to parents, guardians and educators in levels, with rewards as their knowledge grows. The study uses action research with a mixed-methods approach to analyse how gamification can support education and the prevention of childhood violence.
