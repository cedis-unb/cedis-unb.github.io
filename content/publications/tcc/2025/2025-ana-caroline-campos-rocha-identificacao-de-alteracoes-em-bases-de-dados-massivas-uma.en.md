## Abstract

Proposes and develops an extensible approach for monitoring changes to records in large JSON files, providing traceability without relying on traditional databases. The method splits the records of a single file into isolated files, generates content-based identifiers and compares versions through hash functions to identify what changed with each new data load. Tests indicate that the strategy is a solid foundation for tracking the integrity of continuous data streams.
