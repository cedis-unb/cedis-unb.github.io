## Abstract

Investigates how chatbots based on large language models can support the elicitation and discovery of functional requirements by generating user stories from stakeholder information. The mixed-methods research developed a web-based chatbot prototype that asks guided questions based on elicitation techniques and structures the answers as user stories. The system was validated with requirements engineers working on a real project.
