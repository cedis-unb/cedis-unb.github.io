## Abstract

This article presents an experience report on the creation of basic computing teaching resources for older adults. The research used user-centered design to make the resources more accessible, precise, concise and intelligible for these users. The study involved 116 older adults, most of them women, with an average age of 65 and heterogeneous schooling and social class, all of whom completed the outreach project "Oficinas de Informática para a Terceira Idade" between 2008 and 2010 at the Federal University of Santa Catarina. Two teaching resources were developed: a printed handout and two video lessons. The resources were evaluated by the target audience through questionnaires and a series of experiments built together with these users.
