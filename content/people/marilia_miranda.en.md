## {{< i18n "people_profile" >}}
- Current position: Associate Professor at the [University of Brasília](https://www.unb.br/) (UnB), [Faculty of Science and Technology in Engineering](https://fcte.unb.br/) (FCTE).
- Permanent faculty member of the Graduate Programme in Biomedical Engineering (PPGEB) and of the Graduate Programme in Development, Society and International Cooperation (PPGDSCI).
- Areas: probability and statistics, applied statistics and quantitative methods for engineering; population and health, demographic analysis, ageing and mortality.

## {{< i18n "people_background" >}}
- PhD in Demography - Cedeplar, Federal University of Minas Gerais (2011).
- MSc in Demography - Cedeplar, Federal University of Minas Gerais (2008).
- BSc in Statistics - University of Brasília (2005).

## Collaboration with CEDIS
She co-advises work at the intersection of demography and software engineering, focused on population ageing, digital literacy and the quality of digital public services for older adults.
