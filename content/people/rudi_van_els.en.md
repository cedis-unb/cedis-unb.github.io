## {{< i18n "people_profile" >}}
- Current position: Associate Professor at the [University of Brasília](https://www.unb.br/) (UnB), [Faculty of Science and Technology in Engineering](https://fcte.unb.br/) (FCTE), Energy Engineering programme.
- Areas: Electrical Engineering with emphasis on process control and automation; renewable energy, rural electrification and electric mobility.

## {{< i18n "people_background" >}}

- MSc in Electrical Engineering - University of Brasília.
- BSc in Electrical Engineering - Federal University of Maranhão (1990).

## Collaboration with CEDIS
Mentor of the {{< link-interno "/projects/agromart" "AgroMart" >}} project together with {{< link-interno "/people/andre_lanna" "André Lanna" >}} — a platform supporting Community Supported Agriculture (CSA) groups, built through successive undergraduate theses at FCTE/UnB.

He co-advised four of the project's undergraduate theses, all recorded in UnB's digital library of student work:

- 2022 — An evolution of the Agromart project: open source, payment methods and co-farmer management.
- 2023 — An evolution of the Agromart project: individualized and automated deployment of a CSA environment.
- 2023 — Payment integration module and maintenance for the Agromart software.
- 2023 — Association for Agromart applications of a CSA in cloud.
