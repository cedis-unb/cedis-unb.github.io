Brasilia, August 25, 2026

The **FCTE Student Support** software received a **computer program registration certificate** granted by the **Brazilian National Institute of Industrial Property (INPI)**, under process **BR512026003515-5**.

The product was developed by **Giulia Domingues de Alcantara** and **Breno Henrique de Souza**, under the supervision of Prof. {{< link-interno "/people/george_marsicano" "George Marsicano Corrêa" >}}, within the {{< link-interno "/projects/ia_dados_transformacao_digital" "Artificial intelligence and data analysis for digital transformation" >}} project.

The software is a virtual assistant on Telegram that centralizes and streamlines access to administrative and academic information at the **School of Sciences and Engineering Technologies (FCTE/UnB)**. It uses **AI with a RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) approach** supported by a vector knowledge base to retrieve relevant content and generate more accurate natural-language answers. The main goal is to reduce dependence on in-person and registrar service, increase student autonomy, and shorten the time to find essential information (enrollment, internships, SIGAA, hours crediting, etc.).

The product contrasts with static systems, confusing forms, and long searches on SIGAA, offering a simple, accessible, and personalized conversational experience, with optional handoff to a human. In user evaluations, the chatbot was rated more functional and intuitive than the official portal. More information is available on the {{< link-interno "/products/apoio_estudantil_fcte" "product page at CEDIS" >}}.

The INPI registration secures legal protection of the computer program for 50 years, as established by **Law No. 9.609/1998**.
