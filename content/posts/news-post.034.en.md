Brasilia, October 10, 2026

Some software generates profit. Some software generates dignity.

{{< link-interno "/projects/software_bem" "Software for Good" >}} was built for the second kind. Since 23 September 2020, when it was launched at the 20th University Week at UnB, the CEDIS umbrella programme has had one non-negotiable rule: **the solution reaches the end user at no cost**. Six years on, the programme is opening its doors — and there is room for your help.

## What is already running

Not a promise. Working code.

{{< link-interno "/projects/doarti" "DOARTI" >}} was born during the pandemic and today supports **21 social initiatives**, connecting charitable organisations to donors in the Federal District. It is 100% volunteer-built.

{{< link-interno "/projects/agromart" "AgroMart" >}} connects family farmers to the people who buy from them, in Community Supported Agriculture groups. It started at a hackathon and has grown with every undergraduate thesis since 2021, in the open.

LEPIC, with UFMG, measures children's reading fluency so that literacy policy rests on evidence rather than guesswork. {{< link-interno "/projects/programa_a3m" "A3M" >}} brings active methodologies and learning indicators into the classroom. And {{< link-interno "/projects/gsd" "Green SD" >}} takes care of the impact nobody sees: the energy the software itself consumes.

In 2026, two of the programme's applications — {{< link-interno "/products/prevencao_abuso_infantil" "Child Sexual Abuse Prevention" >}} and {{< link-interno "/products/prevencao_violencia_infancia" "Childhood Violence Prevention" >}} — received registration certificates from INPI.

## Who we are looking for

**Students** who want their final thesis to be useful after it is handed in. **Faculty** from any field: the programme is about software, but it is built with social work, health, education and advertising. **Professionals** willing to give a few hours of their experience. And **institutions** with a real social need and no one to turn it into technology.

You do not need to arrive ready. You need to arrive.

## What you take with you

A real project, a multidisciplinary team, a problem with consequences. Publications, hands-on practice, and the chance to see what you built end up in the hands of someone who needs it.

## How to join

Write to Professor {{< link-interno "/people/george_marsicano" "George Marsicano Corrêa" >}}, the programme coordinator:

**georgemarsicano@unb.br**

Tell him who you are and what you would like to do. If you would rather look first, the {{< link-interno "/projects/software_bem" "Software for Good page" >}} sets out the fronts, goals and projects in detail.

The programme is coordinated by George Marsicano, with Professors {{< link-interno "/people/ricardo_ajax" "Ricardo Ajax Dias Kosloski" >}} and {{< link-interno "/people/sergio_freitas" "Sergio Antônio Andrade de Freitas" >}} among its researchers, at CEDIS/FCTE-UnB.
