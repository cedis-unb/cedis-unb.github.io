Brasilia, May 26, 2026

The **Machine Learning Application for Non-Invasive Prediction of Euploidy in Human Embryos Based on Morphokinetic Data** software received a **computer program registration certificate** granted by the **Brazilian National Institute of Industrial Property (INPI)** under process **BR512026003650-0**, published in the **Industrial Property Gazette (RPI) No. 2890, dated May 26, 2026**.

The product was developed by **Maria Eduarda dos Santos Abritta Ferreira** and **Sabrina Caldas Berno**, under the supervision of Prof. {{< link-interno "/people/george_marsicano" "George Marsicano Corrêa" >}}, within the {{< link-interno "/projects/ia_dados_transformacao_digital" "Artificial intelligence and data analysis for digital transformation" >}} project. The registration was granted with **Fundação Universidade de Brasília (UnB)** as the rights holder.

The software uses **Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning** techniques to predict embryonic euploidy in in vitro fertilization treatments, based on the automated analysis of morphokinetic and morphological data obtained from continuous embryonic monitoring systems (Time-Lapse). The model, based on an Artificial Neural Network of the **Multilayer Perceptron (MLP)** type, achieved **88.2% accuracy** and **AUC of 0.944**, and uses the **LIME** method to make predictions interpretable — an essential requirement in the biomedical context.

Intended for assisted reproduction clinics, embryologists, researchers, and fertility centers, the system serves as a clinical decision-support tool for embryo selection. Unlike existing market solutions — which rely mostly on proprietary scores or subjective morphological analysis — the program employs statistically trained and validated models combined with explainability methods. More information is available on the {{< link-interno "/products/predicao_euploidia" "product page at CEDIS" >}}.

The INPI registration secures legal protection of the computer program for 50 years, as established by **Law No. 9.609/1998**.
