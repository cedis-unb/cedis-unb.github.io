Brasilia, August 9, 2026

*Learn. Teach. Connect.* Under that motto, CEDIS presents the **CEDIS Ecosystem**: six integrated products that work together to turn content into meaningful learning experiences, with data and intelligence at the service of teachers and students. Six products, one ecosystem, one mission — turning knowledge into impact.

The family was born in July, with the {{< link-interno "/products/deckmanager" "CEDIS Deck Manager" >}}, and today gains its own visual identity and five new members. Each is an independent application, with its own address and database; they talk to one another through secure APIs, and no service blocks another — if one goes down, the class goes on.

## Who does what

The {{< link-interno "/products/deckmanager" "Deck Manager" >}} is the teacher's cockpit: create, organise, version and publish decks. {{< link-interno "/products/slides" "CEDIS Slides" >}} is where the class happens: decks presented on any device, with activities and real-time answers. {{< link-interno "/products/concept_studio" "Concept Studio" >}} is the studio of concepts — maps, relations and questionnaires that reach students inside Slides. The {{< link-interno "/products/atlas" "Atlas do Conhecimento" >}} (Knowledge Atlas) is the student's journey: progress along four dimensions, routes, stamps and constellations. The {{< link-interno "/products/passport" "Learning Passport" >}} is the pseudonymous identity that records all of it without an e-mail or any personal data. And {{< link-interno "/products/cedis_learning_analytics" "Learning Analytics" >}} works backstage, turning signals from the whole ecosystem into indicators and insights.

## The journey in seven steps

The teacher creates the content in the Deck Manager, delivers it in Slides, explores concepts and applies questionnaires in Concept Studio; students move forward in the Atlas, with their learning recorded in the Passport; Learning Analytics aggregates and correlates; the teacher decides better and supports each student. What the ecosystem is after is simple to state: more learning, more engagement, and pedagogical decisions grounded in data.

## Privacy first

No product in the family stores an e-mail or identifiable personal data by default. The student's identity is pseudonymous; there is no public ranking; every aggregate respects k-anonymity; and it is the student who decides what to share — and when to erase everything. The LGPD is not a banner here: it is the design.
