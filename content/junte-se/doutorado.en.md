The doctorate supervised by CEDIS researchers is the **Professional Doctorate in Applied Computing**, offered by the Graduate Programme in Applied Computing (PPCA/UnB). It is not a full-time academic PhD: the course is designed for master's degree holders who already work in the field and want to research a problem from their own job.

The admission calls and all admission rules are the responsibility of PPCA, which runs the selection. A CEDIS researcher supervises those who are admitted, but does not set the criteria and cannot admit candidates directly. Please check the criteria below **before** writing to a potential supervisor.

**Please note:** this page summarises PPCA Call no. 05/2026. Criteria may change with each call, and the official source is always the [PPCA website](https://ppca.unb.br/) (in Portuguese). Questions about the call and the admission rules should be addressed to PPCA.

## Check whether the programme is for you

- **Completed master's degree.** If you are finishing your master's, you may apply as long as you complete it at least one month before the course starts.
- **Employment.** Places are for professionals who work in an IT or risk management unit and are civil servants or employees of a public or private institution or company.
- **Employer release.** The application requires a letter from the head of your organisation, stating its Brazilian company registration number (CNPJ), committing to release you on Fridays and Saturdays for 24 months and approving the topic of your pre-project.
- **In-person classes in Brasília.** All classes are taught in person at ICC Centro, Darcy Ribeiro Campus, on Fridays and Saturdays. There is no distance-learning option.
- **Residence in Brazil.** Only candidates residing in Brazil may apply.
- **Cybersecurity line.** The doctorate has a single research line, Cybersecurity, with three axes: Data Science, Software Engineering and Risk Management. The pre-project must name the axis and detail the security failures associated with the problem.
- **English.** You must show reading ability in English, at application or by the end of the fourth semester. Proficiency is required to defend the thesis.

## If you live outside Brazil

The call only accepts candidates residing in Brazil, and the course has no distance-learning option. If you live abroad, you cannot apply from there: you would need to live in Brazil, be employed with your employer's release, and attend classes in Brasília.

Foreign nationals who already live in Brazil may apply. The call asks for the Brazilian foreigner registration (RNE) or a passport with a visa.

If you want to collaborate with CEDIS without joining the programme, see the {{< link-interno "/junte-se/parcerias" "Institutional partnerships" >}} path.

## What the course is like

- The course lasts 48 months. Students are expected to complete the 36 credits in the first 24 months.
- Credits are split between core courses, courses from the chosen axis, electives and two seminars.
- To graduate, you must pass the qualifying exam and the thesis defence and have published at least one paper in a core Computer Science journal, as defined by PPCA.
- Those who took their master's at PPCA can transfer credits from the core courses and from the line they studied.

## How selection works

- When applying, you choose **one** axis and submit a pre-project of 8 to 12 pages, plus two letters of recommendation.
- The stages are an online interview, assessment of the pre-project, and analysis of your Lattes CV. The interview and the pre-project are eliminatory, with a minimum mark of 60.
- In the final mark, the pre-project carries weight 5, the interview weight 4 and the CV weight 1. The minimum final mark is 70.
- Call no. 05/2026 offered 10 places: 7 open to all eligible professionals, 2 for Black candidates and 1 for UnB staff, with applications from 9 November to 8 December 2026 and intake in the first semester of 2027.
- The application fee was R$ 850.00, with the exemptions set out in the call. The call does not mention scholarships.

## How to start

1. Read the most recent doctorate call on the [PPCA website](https://ppca.unb.br/category/editais/) and check that you meet the criteria.
2. Talk to your manager about the release letter and about the topic: the research problem should come from your job.
3. Study recent CEDIS projects and publications, and write to a potential supervisor explaining where you work, what problem you want to investigate and which axis you plan to apply for.
4. Prepare the pre-project in the format the call requires, request the two letters of recommendation and apply through UnB's system.

Want to get to know the programme first? PPCA also opens a call for **special students**, who take individual courses, also in person. Those later admitted as regular doctoral students can transfer up to 18 credits.
