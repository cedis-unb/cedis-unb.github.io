The master's degree supervised by CEDIS researchers is the **Professional Master's in Applied Computing**, offered by the Graduate Programme in Applied Computing (PPCA/UnB). It is not a full-time academic master's: the course is designed for people who already work in the field and want to research a problem from their own job.

Students are selected by PPCA, through its own public call. A CEDIS researcher supervises those who are admitted, but cannot admit candidates directly. Please check the criteria below **before** writing to a potential supervisor.

**Please note:** this page summarises PPCA Call no. 01/2026. Criteria may change with each call, and the official source is always the [PPCA website](https://ppca.unb.br/) (in Portuguese).

## Check whether the programme is for you

- **Employment.** Places are for professionals who work in an IT or risk management unit and are civil servants or employees of a public or private institution or company.
- **Employer release.** The application requires a letter from the head of your organisation, stating its Brazilian company registration number (CNPJ), committing to release you on Fridays and Saturdays for 24 months and approving the topic of your pre-project.
- **In-person classes in Brasília.** Courses are taught in person at the Darcy Ribeiro Campus: Fridays from 8:00 to 12:30 and from 14:00 to 18:30, and Saturdays from 8:00 to 12:30. There is no distance-learning option.
- **Residence in Brazil.** Only candidates residing in Brazil may apply.
- **Degree.** Your undergraduate degree must be completed before the course starts. A degree obtained abroad must be revalidated or recognised in Brazil.
- **English.** You must show reading ability in English, at application or by the end of the second semester. Proficiency is required to defend the dissertation.

## If you live outside Brazil

The call only accepts candidates residing in Brazil, and the course has no distance-learning option. If you live abroad, you cannot apply from there: you would need to live in Brazil, be employed with your employer's release, and attend classes in Brasília.

Foreign nationals who already live in Brazil may apply. The call asks for the Brazilian foreigner registration (RNE) or a passport with a visa, and the interview assesses, among other points, correct use of Portuguese.

If you want to collaborate with CEDIS without joining the programme, see the {{< link-interno "/junte-se/parcerias" "Institutional partnerships" >}} path.

## What the course is like

- There is one intake per year. In the 2026 call, applications were in May and June, for a start in the second semester.
- The course lasts 18 to 24 months, with the qualifying exam by the 12th month.
- It has 24 credits: six core courses and six from the chosen research line, which can be Data Science, Software Engineering or Risk Management.
- The dissertation investigates a real problem from the student's professional work.

## How selection works

- When applying, you choose **one** research line and submit a pre-project of 5 to 8 pages.
- The stages are an online interview, assessment of the pre-project, and analysis of your Lattes CV and academic transcript. The interview and the pre-project are eliminatory, with a minimum mark of 60.
- In the final mark, the interview and the pre-project carry weight 6, and the CV and transcript carry weight 4.
- The 2026 call offered 30 places: 18 reserved for staff of PGDF and Banco do Brasil, and 12 open to all eligible professionals.
- The application fee was R$ 850.00, with the exemptions set out in the call. The call does not mention scholarships.

## Where CEDIS comes in

Two CEDIS researchers are on the PPCA faculty, both in the **Software Engineering** line: {{< link-interno "/people/sergio_freitas" "Sergio Freitas" >}} and {{< link-interno "/people/george_marsicano" "George Marsicano" >}}. Topics in that line in the call include gamification and software engineering, artificial intelligence and software engineering, human-computer interaction, requirements engineering, learning management systems and digital transformation.

The full faculty list is on the [PPCA faculty page](https://ppca.unb.br/corpo-docente/) (in Portuguese).

## How to start

1. Read the most recent master's call on the [PPCA website](https://ppca.unb.br/category/editais/) and check that you meet the criteria.
2. Talk to your manager about the release letter and about the topic: the research problem should come from your job.
3. Identify a CEDIS researcher whose topics match yours, and write explaining where you work, what problem you want to investigate and which research line you plan to apply for.
4. Prepare the pre-project in the format the call requires and apply through UnB's system.

Want to get to know the programme first? PPCA also opens a call for **special students**, who take individual courses, also in person. Those later admitted as regular students can transfer up to 12 credits.
