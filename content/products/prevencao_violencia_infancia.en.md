## Product Description

**Childhood Violence Prevention** is a technological product recorded in 2025 in the Lattes CV of Prof. {{< link-interno "/people/george_marsicano" "George Marsicano Corrêa" >}}. The software is a gamified mobile application built on the **Good Practices Manual** of the "Pró-vida e Eu Me Protejo" project — a partnership between the Pró-Vida institution and the Eu Me Protejo project — to increase engagement and facilitate access to guidance content for parents, guardians, and educators on preventing violence in childhood, including in offline mode.

## INPI Registration

In **2026**, the software received a **computer program registration certificate** granted by **INPI** under process **BR512026005120-7**. The registration secures legal protection for 50 years, as established by **Law No. 9.609/1998**, and reinforces the institutional recognition of the technological output associated with the {{< link-interno "/projects/software_bem" "Software for Good" >}} program. More details are available in the {{< link-interno "/posts/news-post.032" "news item about the registration" >}}.

## Main Function

The application delivers the manual's content in an interactive, progress-oriented format, with gamification mechanics (quizzes, XP, badges, and simulated scenarios), allowing the user to progress through stages and receive rewards while learning. It operates with offline access: after download and installation, the app works without internet and preserves user progress even when the app is closed.

## Social Context and Differentiators

The work stems from the understanding that childhood violence is a serious topic and that, despite existing initiatives, there are still awareness gaps — many parents and guardians hesitate or struggle to address the subject with children. The "Pró-vida e Eu Me Protejo" project exists to support prevention with pedagogical materials, and this application amplifies the reach and engagement of that content through technology and gamification. Unlike the traditional PDF-based consumption of the manual, the gamified offline experience increases engagement and accessibility.

## Technology

- **Platform:** mobile application, initially distributed on Android via APK (internal use, not published on stores).
- **Stack:** React Native + TypeScript.
- **Build:** EAS Build (Expo Application Services) to generate the APK.
- **Distribution:** direct link for installation on test devices; after functional validation, moved to data collection with the target audience.
- **Non-functional quality:** offline-first with progress persistence, for use in contexts with limited connectivity.
- **Process:** high-fidelity prototyping in Figma; MVP built with agile practices (Scrum/XP).

## Future Directions

Formal Google Play publication with a signed, versioned release pipeline; content and track evolution (new manual modules, simulated scenarios, and quizzes); and optional synchronization (without breaking the offline requirement) to monitor effectiveness and evolution of usage when internet is available.
